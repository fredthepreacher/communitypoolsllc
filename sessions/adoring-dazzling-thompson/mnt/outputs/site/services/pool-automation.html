Automation Brands We Install & Service

Community Pools LLC works with all major pool automation platforms. We recommend the system that best matches your existing equipment and budget:

Pentair IntelliConnect, EasyTouch & SunTouch — Pentair's automation lineup ranges from the entry-level IntelliConnect (ideal for simple variable speed pump and light control) to the full-featured EasyTouch for complete multi-feature pools. Pentair's ScreenLogic app provides excellent remote access and scheduling.

Hayward OmniLogic & ProLogic — Hayward's OmniLogic is one of the most capable and user-friendly automation platforms available, with a large touchscreen control panel and comprehensive Hayward Home app. ProLogic provides full-featured control for complex equipment configurations.

Jandy AquaLink RS & iAquaLink — Jandy's AquaLink system is widely installed throughout Southwest Florida and offers reliable automation with iAquaLink remote access via smartphone. AquaLink is a popular upgrade path for many older pools.

If you already have equipment from one manufacturer, we'll generally recommend that brand's automation platform for seamless compatibility and single-brand support.
