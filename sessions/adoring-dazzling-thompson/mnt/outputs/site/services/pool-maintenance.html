What Pool Maintenance Covers

Pool maintenance goes beyond cleaning — it's the ongoing management of your pool's mechanical systems, water chemistry, and structural health. Community Pools LLC's maintenance program covers every aspect of pool operation so nothing falls through the cracks.

🔧

Filter Service

Cartridge cleaning, DE grid inspection, and backwashing to maintain filtration efficiency and water clarity.

⚙

Pump & Equipment Inspection

Regular equipment checks to catch emerging issues — motor noise, pressure changes, leaks — before they become failures.

🧪

Water Chemistry Management

Full chemistry testing and balancing at every visit — pH, chlorine, alkalinity, stabilizer, calcium hardness.

👒

Surface & Basket Service

Skimming, brushing, vacuuming, and basket clearing as part of every complete maintenance visit.

📋

Service Documentation

Visit records with chemistry readings and equipment notes after every appointment — so you always know what was done.

📞

Issue Communication

Direct communication of any equipment concerns or water quality issues found — with honest repair recommendations.
