Gas Heater vs. Heat Pump in Southwest Florida

Southwest Florida pool owners typically choose between two heater types: natural gas/propane heaters and electric heat pumps. Each has distinct advantages depending on how you use your pool.

Gas heaters heat pools quickly — from cold to swim temperature in 1–2 hours — making them ideal for pools that aren't heated continuously or for spas that need fast heat-up. They perform regardless of air temperature, which matters during brief Florida cold snaps. The downside is higher operating cost when run continuously.

Heat pump heaters are extremely energy-efficient because they extract heat from the air rather than generating it through combustion. In Florida's warm climate, heat pumps operate effectively most of the year and cost 50–75% less to run than gas heaters when used for continuous heating. They heat more slowly than gas, but for pools maintained at a consistent temperature, this doesn't matter.

Many Southwest Florida homeowners with both a pool and spa use a gas heater for the spa (fast heat-up) and either skip heating the pool or use a heat pump for the main pool. Community Pools LLC will help you evaluate which option makes sense for your specific usage patterns and budget.
