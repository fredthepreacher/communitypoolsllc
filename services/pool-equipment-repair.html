Frequently Asked Questions About Equipment Repair

A well-maintained pool pump in Florida typically lasts 8–12 years. Florida's year-round operation means Florida pumps run more total hours than pumps in seasonal climates, which accelerates wear.

Repair is generally worth it if the unit is less than 8–10 years old and the repair cost is under 40–50% of a new heater. Heat exchangers corroded through due to improper water chemistry are typically not worth repairing.

A variable speed pump typically reduces pool pump electricity costs by 60–80%. A single-speed 1.5 HP pump running 8 hours/day costs roughly $80–$120/month in Florida. The same task via variable speed typically costs $15–$30/month.

Common causes: a tripped circuit breaker, control board failure, failed communication wire, firmware issue, or lightning damage. Lightning is a frequent cause of automation failures in Florida.
