Why You Need a Licensed Pool Repair Contractor in Florida

Florida law requires a Certified Pool/Spa Contractor (CPC) license for pool repairs. Community Pools LLC holds license CPC1459841 issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

Unlicensed pool repair operators are common in Southwest Florida. Their work creates liability for the homeowner, may void equipment warranties, and frequently introduces new problems. We provide written estimates before beginning any repair work — no surprise charges and no upselling of parts you don't need.

Frequently Asked Questions About Pool Repair

A pump warrants repair if it's less than 5–7 years old and repair cost is under 50% of a new pump. Replacement is better when the motor is over 8 years old or has repeated failures.

Loud pump noise usually indicates air in the system, a failing bearing, a clogged impeller, or loose mounting hardware. Each has a different fix. Call 941.258.7529 for a diagnosis.

Simple repairs can often be done in 1–2 hours. More complex repairs requiring special-order parts or permits may take 3–7 business days. We provide time estimates alongside cost estimates.

Many above-ground plumbing repairs can be completed without draining. Underground pipe repairs may require locating the break precisely and may require partial draining for access.
