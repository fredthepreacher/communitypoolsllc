Green Pool Recovery: What We Do

A green pool is not just an aesthetic problem — it's a safety hazard. Algae-laden water provides ideal conditions for harmful bacteria including E. coli and Pseudomonas. You cannot see the bottom of a green pool, which creates a drowning risk. And the longer it sits, the harder and more expensive the recovery becomes.

Community Pools LLC provides professional green pool recovery service throughout Southwest Florida. We don't just shock the water and hope — we identify the reason the pool went green, address the root cause as part of the treatment, and put a prevention plan in place so it doesn't happen again.

Why Florida Pools Go Green

Southwest Florida has the highest rate of green pool incidents in the country. Chlorine depletes faster here due to intense UV radiation and heavy wet-season rainfall diluting cyanuric acid — the UV stabilizer for chlorine. In Florida's summer water temperatures, a pool can go from clear to fully green in 48–72 hours once chlorine is depleted.

The most common specific causes of green pools in Port Charlotte: missed service visits (especially in summer), equipment failures that weren't caught promptly, storm events that introduce massive organic load, and seasonal residents returning to pools that weren't on a proper caretaker plan.
