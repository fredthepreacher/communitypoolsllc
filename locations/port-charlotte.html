Pool Care Challenges Specific to Port Charlotte

Port Charlotte pools age differently than pools in other parts of Florida because of how the community was built. General Development Corporation's original master plan created a sprawling, flat grid of residential lots with underground utility infrastructure. Pools built on that original grid have now been in the ground for 30–50 years in many neighborhoods. The sandy soil that characterizes Charlotte County can shift and settle over decades, and underground pool plumbing — particularly the return and suction lines — is subject to gradual movement that can create joint failures and slow leaks over time.

Port Charlotte's water supply is also worth noting for pool chemistry management. The water has moderate to high calcium hardness, which accelerates scale formation on pool surfaces and salt cells if pH is not kept in the correct range. Managing calcium hardness is a routine part of every chemistry visit for our Port Charlotte customers — it's one of the key factors that determines how long your plaster surface lasts before it needs replastering.

Seasonal residents are a significant portion of Port Charlotte's pool-owning population. A large number of homeowners spend May through October or longer in northern states, leaving their pools in need of reliable caretaker service. Community Pools LLC has specific experience managing pools through Port Charlotte's summer wet season when owners are away — including post-hurricane assessment visits after significant storm events that affect Charlotte County.

For pool renovations in Port Charlotte, travertine paver decks have become the dominant upgrade choice among homeowners renovating their pool surrounds. The original concrete decks installed in the 1970s–90s have cracked and scaled extensively in many neighborhoods, and travertine's cooler surface temperature and superior durability make it the preferred replacement. Community Pools LLC has completed numerous travertine deck projects throughout Port Charlotte — examples of our work are in our project gallery.
