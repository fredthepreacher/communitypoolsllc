Rotonda West’s Pool Service Needs

Rotonda West pools present some specific characteristics worth understanding. The community was developed across several decades, and the pool inventory reflects that range — some pools date to the 1970s and early 80s in the first areas developed, while others were built in the 2000s and even recently in Rotonda's newer sections. This age range means service needs vary significantly by neighborhood: older pools may need more frequent equipment checks and may be approaching renovation age, while newer pools primarily need proper chemistry management and preventive maintenance.

Rotonda West's water supply has specific chemistry characteristics, including calcium hardness levels that require attention to scaling prevention, particularly for pools with salt systems. Scale formation on salt cells is an ongoing concern for Rotonda West saltwater pool owners, and our service protocol includes cell inspection and cleaning at appropriate intervals to maintain output and extend cell life.

The golf course environment that surrounds much of Rotonda West contributes to pool chemistry through fertilizer runoff, particularly during and after irrigation and heavy rain. Phosphate levels in Rotonda West pools can be elevated relative to non-golf-adjacent communities, and phosphate management is a regular part of our service approach for pools in golf course-adjacent neighborhoods. High phosphate levels make pools significantly more susceptible to algae outbreaks at normal chlorine concentrations.

For seasonal caretaker customers, Rotonda West's hurricane exposure is a meaningful consideration. The community sits in a zone regularly affected by Gulf storms, and post-hurricane assessment visits are an important part of our caretaker service commitment. We monitor storm tracks and schedule assessment visits as soon as safe access is possible after significant weather events to clear debris, restore chemistry, and verify equipment operation.

Why do Rotonda West pools get high phosphate levels?

Rotonda West's five golf courses use fertilizer programs that, through irrigation runoff and rainfall, can elevate phosphate levels in pools adjacent to golf course areas. Phosphates are algae's primary nutrient source — high phosphate pools are significantly more susceptible to algae outbreaks even when chlorine is at normal levels, because algae can thrive at lower chlorine concentrations when abundant phosphates are present. We test and treat phosphate levels as part of our regular service visits for Rotonda West pools.

My Rotonda West pool is from the 1980s or 90s — is it time to renovate?

Many 1980s–90s Rotonda West pools are at or approaching renovation age. Key indicators: plaster that feels rough or shows consistent staining despite correct chemistry; concrete decks with significant cracking or trip hazards; equipment requiring increasingly frequent repairs; or a pool that simply looks dated.
